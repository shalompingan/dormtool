About DormTool

We are a team of data-driven creators dedicated to removing the friction from college move-ins, independent living, and personal finance transitions. Our personal experience — wrestling with forgotten dorm essentials, splitting utility bills with roommates, calculating rent on a student budget, and navigating the chaos of moving out — inspired us to build the tools we wish we had.

Every micro-utility on dormtool.com was built with a single philosophy: one problem, one page, zero fluff. No signups, no data collection, no accounts. You open the page, you get the answer, you move on with your day.

Our Mission

To equip students and young adults with smart, free, private tools that turn life's most stressful transitions into manageable checklists. Whether you are packing for your first dorm room, calculating whether you can afford that off-campus apartment, or coordinating a move-out with three roommates — we have your back.

How We Build

Behind the code, we are builders who believe the web should still be lightweight, fast, and accessible to everyone. Every tool on this site is a single HTML file — no frameworks, no databases, no bloat. Just clean, vanilla JavaScript and CSS that works on any device, anywhere in the world. All processing happens in your browser; your data never touches a server.

Our Story

DormTool started as a side project — a simple checklist for a friend moving into their first dorm room. That checklist became a laundry hub, then a move-out guide, then a rent calculator, and before long we realized there was a whole ecosystem of "adulting" tools that students needed but couldn't find in one place without signing up for something.

We decided to build them all — free, fast, and private. Today DormTool is used by thousands of students across the US and continues to grow as we add new tools and guides for every stage of the college journey.

What We Believe

Students shouldn't need to create an account to figure out their GPA. You shouldn't have to sit through a webinar to estimate moving costs. And you definitely shouldn't need a spreadsheet to split a utility bill three ways. We believe the best tools are the ones you use once and forget about — because they solved your problem and got out of your way.

Thanks for stopping by. Now go check something off your list.

— The DormTool Team

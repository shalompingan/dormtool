How It Works

This calculator uses the weighted average formula: Required Final Score = (Target Grade − Current Grade × (1 − Final Weight)) ÷ Final Weight. Results are estimates — always verify with your instructor's grading policy.

How to Use This Final Grade Calculator

This calculator tells you exactly what score you need on your final exam to reach your target course grade. Follow these steps:

  • Enter your current grade — Input your current percentage grade in the course before the final exam. This should include all assignments, quizzes, and midterms.
  • Enter the final exam weight — What percentage of your total grade does the final exam count for? This is usually listed in your course syllabus.
  • Set your target grade — Enter the overall percentage grade you want to achieve in the course, then click "Calculate My Final Grade". Your required score will appear instantly.

The formula: Required Final Score = (Target Grade − Current Grade × (1 − Final Weight)) ÷ Final Weight. For example, if you have 85% currently, the final is worth 30%, and you want 90%: (90 − 85 × 0.7) ÷ 0.3 = 101.7%, meaning you'd need near-perfect performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I need over 100% on my final?

If the calculator shows a score above 100%, your target grade may be mathematically impossible given your current grade and the final's weight. You can either adjust your target to a lower grade or focus on extra credit opportunities to boost your current grade before the final.

What does "Already Secured" mean?

If the result says "Already Secured," it means your current grade is already high enough to reach your target even if you scored 0% on the final. In this case, the final can only improve your grade further — there's no pressure!

How accurate is this calculator?

This calculator uses the standard weighted average formula and is accurate as long as your input numbers are correct. Always double-check your final exam weight and current grade against your course syllabus, as different instructors may use slightly different grading schemes.

What target grade should I set?

Many students aim for a 90%+ (A-) to keep a strong GPA. If you're in a challenging course, a realistic target might be 80-85% (B to B+). Use the What-If tab to see how different final scores affect your overall grade at various target levels.

Does this work for any grading system?

Yes — the formula works for any percentage-based grading system. If your school uses letter grades without percentages, convert them first: A = 90-100%, B = 80-89%, C = 70-79%, etc. The What-If table also shows letter grade equivalents for each possible final exam score.

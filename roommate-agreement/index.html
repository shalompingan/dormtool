How the Dorm Roommate Agreement Generator Works

Living with a roommate for the first time can be challenging. This interactive agreement tool helps you and your roommate discuss and agree on important topics before conflicts arise. Instead of awkward conversations later, set clear expectations now.

Who Should Use This

This tool is designed for incoming college freshmen moving into a dorm for the first time, returning students with new roommates, or anyone living in shared on-campus housing. It covers the most common sources of roommate conflict: money, cleaning, guests, noise, sharing, and communication.

How It Works

Browse each category tab and select the option that works best for both of you. Some topics like rent split or quiet hours are straightforward; others like guest policies may need more discussion. Take turns picking options and compromise where you disagree. Once all questions are answered, enter your names and generate a printable agreement you can both sign and keep on your phone or desk.

Tips for a Successful Roommate Agreement

Be honest during the process — this agreement only works if both of you feel heard. Schedule a 30-minute block to fill it out together during move-in week. Keep the signed PDF on your phone so you can reference it later. And remember: this is a living document. If something isn't working, revisit it and adjust.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this roommate agreement legally binding?

This agreement is a mutual understanding between roommates, not a legally enforceable contract. It is designed to prevent conflicts by setting clear expectations, not to replace a formal lease or university housing agreement. For serious issues involving rent or property, consult your university housing office.

Do I need to fill out every category?

We recommend discussing all categories, but you can skip any that don't apply to your situation. The agreement will only include the categories you complete. If a topic feels unnecessary for your specific roommate dynamic, leave it blank.

Can I save my progress and come back?

Yes. Your selections are automatically saved to your browser's local storage. Close the page and return anytime — your answers will be exactly where you left them. Your data never leaves your device.

How do I print or share the agreement?

After generating the agreement, click the "Print Agreement" button to open your browser's print dialog. You can save it as a PDF, print it on paper, or use the "Copy to Clipboard" button to paste it into a Google Doc or group chat.

Can I use this for off-campus apartments too?

Yes. While the language is geared toward dorm living, most topics apply to any shared living situation. For off-campus apartments with formal leases, we recommend also reviewing your lease terms and consulting your landlord for any clauses about subletting or guests.

How to Use This First Apartment Checklist to Move In Without Stress

Moving into your first apartment is exciting, but the list of things you need can feel overwhelming. This checklist helps you track every essential item — from bedroom furniture to cleaning supplies — so you don't forget anything or blow your budget.

Who Should Use This Checklist

This checklist is built for first-time renters, college students moving off campus, and anyone furnishing an apartment from scratch. Whether you're moving into a studio or a two-bedroom with roommates, these categories cover the essentials you'll need on day one and in the first month.

How to Use the First Apartment Checklist

Browse through each category — Bedroom, Kitchen, Living Room, Bathroom, Cleaning, Tools, Utilities, and Food & Pantry. Check off items as you buy or pack them. Use the priority filter to focus on must-haves first. Each item shows a price tier (Budget, Moderate, or Premium) to help you decide where to spend and where to save.

Tips for Budgeting Your First Apartment

Set a total budget before you start shopping. The estimated total at the top updates as you check items off. Focus on Budget-tier items for your first week — you can always upgrade later. Consider buying secondhand for big-ticket items like sofas and dressers; Facebook Marketplace and thrift stores are great places to start.

What to Prioritize During Move-In

Your first week should focus on the bedroom (mattress, sheets, pillows), kitchen (cookware, plates, utensils), and bathroom (shower curtain, towels, toilet paper). These are the areas you'll use immediately. Living room furniture and decor can wait until your second week or first month.

First Apartment Checklist — Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I budget for first apartment essentials?

Most first-time renters spend $800–$1,500 on essential items for a one-bedroom apartment. This includes furniture, kitchen supplies, cleaning tools, and bathroom essentials. Your actual costs depend on whether you buy new or secondhand and how many items you already own.

What items should I buy first?

Prioritize sleep essentials (mattress, sheets, pillows), kitchen basics (plates, utensils, cookware), and bathroom necessities (shower curtain, towels, toilet paper). These are the things you'll need on your very first night. Everything else can wait a few days or weeks.

Can I save my checklist progress?

Yes. Your checked items and custom additions are saved automatically in your browser using localStorage. Your data stays on your device and is never sent to any server. You can close the page and come back anytime — your progress will still be there.

Should I buy everything before moving in?

Not necessarily. Buy the essentials before move-in day, but leave decorative items and non-essentials for after you've settled in. Live in the space for a week first — you'll have a much better sense of what you actually need and where to put things.

How do I choose between budget, moderate, and premium items?

Choose Budget for items that are easy to upgrade later (like towels, utensil sets, and storage containers). Choose Moderate for things you use daily that affect comfort (like cookware, pillows, and lighting). Choose Premium only for long-term investments like a mattress or sofa — these are worth spending more on.

What's the best way to save money when furnishing my first apartment?

Buy secondhand for big furniture pieces (sofas, dressers, coffee tables), shop end-of-season sales for bedding and kitchen items, use the "Shop ↗" buttons on this checklist to compare prices on Amazon, and split shared items like cleaning supplies with roommates.

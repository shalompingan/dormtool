About This Calculator

This calculator uses the standard 4.0 weighted GPA scale. Grades range from A (4.0) to F (0.0). Your GPA is calculated as the sum of (grade points × credits) divided by total credits. Always verify with your school's grading policy — some institutions use plus/minus differently.

How to Plan Your Dorm Shopping Budget

Moving into a dorm for the first time comes with a long list of things to buy — bedding, bathroom supplies, laundry essentials, electronics, and more. Without a plan, costs add up fast. That's where this budget calculator helps.

Who Should Use This Calculator

Whether you're an incoming freshman shopping for your first dorm, a parent helping your child prepare for move-in day, or a returning student looking to replace worn-out items, this tool gives you a clear picture of what you'll spend before you step into a store.

How to Use It

Browse through the categories below and check the items you plan to buy. Each item comes with a default price based on current 2026 retail averages, but you can adjust both the price and quantity to match your actual shopping list. Your total updates instantly at the top of the page, so you always know where you stand against your budget.

Setting a Realistic Budget

A typical dorm shopping budget falls between $300 and $800 depending on how many items you need, whether you're splitting shared purchases with a roommate, and whether you already own some essentials like bedding or a backpack. Start by checking only the essentials, then add extras as your budget allows.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I budget for dorm essentials?

Most students spend between $300 and $800 on dorm move-in shopping. A basic setup (bedding, bathroom supplies, laundry basics, school supplies) typically runs $300-500. Adding tech items, decor, and shared appliances like a mini-fridge can push the total toward $700-800.

What if my total exceeds my budget?

Start by identifying items you can buy after move-in rather than before. Decor, extra storage, and non-essential electronics can wait. You can also split shared items like a mini-fridge, microwave, or rug with your roommate to cut your individual cost by 50%.

Are the prices in this calculator accurate for 2026?

The default prices reflect average retail costs for the 2026-2027 academic year based on major retailers like Amazon, Target, and Walmart. Actual prices vary by brand, location, and sales timing. We recommend checking current prices before you buy and adjusting the values in the calculator accordingly.

Can I save my list and come back later?

Yes. Your selections, quantities, and price adjustments are automatically saved to your browser's local storage. Close the page and come back anytime — your list will be exactly as you left it. This data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.

Should I coordinate with my roommate before shopping?

Absolutely. Dorm rooms are small, and duplicating large items wastes both money and space. Talk to your roommate about who brings the mini-fridge, microwave, TV, rug, and cleaning supplies. Splitting just 3-4 shared items can save each person $100-200.

What items do freshmen most often forget?

Based on student surveys, the most commonly forgotten items include: a mattress protector (dorm mattresses are not new), a small tool kit (screwdriver and scissors), a 10-foot phone charger, shower flip-flops (essential for shared bathrooms), and command strips (most dorms ban nails and tape on walls).

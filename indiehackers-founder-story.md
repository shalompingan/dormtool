# I built DormTool because my freshman move-in was a disaster

I showed up to college with two suitcases, a trash bag full of hangers, and a mini fridge I carried up three flights of stairs by myself.

My roommate brought a backpack.

We had never talked before. Not once. We figured out the hard way that he went to bed at 9pm and I went to bed at 2am. That lasted about two weeks before it got awkward.

That was a few years ago. Last summer I was helping my cousin pack for her first year and realized — nothing has changed. Same generic checklists from colleges. Same "good luck figure it out" vibe.

So I started building stuff.

## What I made

[DormTool](https://dormtool.com/) is a collection of free tools for college students. No signups. No data collection. Just single-page tools that do one thing and get out of your way.

Right now there's:
- A **dorm checklist** that tracks what you've packed and what you still need
- A **roommate agreement generator** so you don't have the 2am bedtime argument I had
- A **bill splitter** for off-campus rent and utilities
- A **rent affordability calculator** so you know if you can actually afford that apartment
- A **student loan calculator** because nobody explains interest
- A **GPA calculator** for when you need to figure out what grade you need on the final

And a few more. All free.

## Why not a mobile app

Because you don't want to download an app you'll use once. These are web tools. Open the page, get your answer, close it. Nothing to install.

## Is it making money?

Not really. There's Amazon affiliate links on the checklist (if you click "Shop" it takes you to Amazon). It covers hosting. That's about it.

## What's next

More tools. A move-out checklist. A first-apartment checklist. Stuff I wished existed when I was figuring this out.

If you're a student — or know one — send them the link. It's [dormtool.com](https://dormtool.com/). Free, no signup, works on any device.

I'm just trying to make move-in day slightly less terrible for the next person.
